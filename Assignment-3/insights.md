# Insights- Superstore Sales Analysis

## Business Insights

- **Customer Revenue Concentration**: The top 5 customers represent **$88,281.80** (3.8% of the total **$2.29M** revenue), highlighting that high-value buyers significantly influence total sales.
- **Revenue Midpoint**: The average total sales value per customer is **$2,896.85**. **37.1% (294 out of 793)** of the customer base accounts for sales above this average.
- **Acquisition vs. Retention**: Only **1.5% (12 customers)** placed exactly one order. A high customer retention rate of **98.5%** suggests strong repeat purchasing behavior.
- **Top Order Sales**: Sean Miller placed the single highest total order value of **$23,661.228**, followed closely by Tamara Chand with **$18,336.740**.
